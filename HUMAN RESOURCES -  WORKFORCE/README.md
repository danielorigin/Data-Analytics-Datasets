**Demographics, Compensation, Performance & Employee Engagement — 2018–2025**

## About the Dataset

This synthetic workforce dataset was created to simulate **real-world employee and human resources records** and provide a practical dataset for learning, analysis, visualization, and portfolio development.

It contains employee-level records covering **demographics, compensation, job roles, departments, performance, training, attendance, engagement, retention, and workforce characteristics**.

The dataset is designed to support practical analytics across **workforce planning, compensation, performance management, employee engagement, retention, training effectiveness, and diversity & inclusion**.

The dataset is suitable for:

**Excel · SQL · Python/Pandas · Power BI · Tableau · R/RStudio**

---

## Context

Access to real-world employee data can be difficult because of **employee privacy, confidentiality, employment regulations, and data protection requirements**.

This dataset was created as a safe synthetic alternative for students, analysts, HR professionals, data professionals, and portfolio developers who want to practice working with realistic workforce data.

The dataset represents employees working for a **German company with an international workforce**, providing opportunities to analyze both domestic and international workforce characteristics.

## Data Quality

The dataset intentionally contains some realistic data-quality challenges, making it useful for demonstrating a complete professional workforce analytics workflow.

### Quality Issues

The dataset includes approximately:

* **2–5% missing values** in selected fields such as phone numbers and certifications
* **1.3% duplicate employee records**
* Salary outliers exceeding **€1 million**
* Attendance values exceeding **100%**
* Missing emergency contact information
* Missing bank details
* Inconsistent gender formats
* Variations in employment type values
* Other unusual records requiring investigation

Salary values and attendance figures should be investigated against appropriate business rules rather than automatically removed.

## Important Note

> **This is a fully synthetic dataset created for educational, analytical, practice, and portfolio purposes.**

It does **not** contain real employee records, personal employment information, real salaries, real bank details, or confidential company information.

Although the dataset simulates a **German company with international employees**, all employee identities, compensation figures, workforce records, and other information are artificially generated.

The dataset should not be used to make real-world decisions regarding **employment, recruitment, compensation, promotion, termination, performance evaluation, employee risk, or other HR decisions**.

Workforce analytics involving demographic or other sensitive attributes should be handled responsibly and evaluated for potential bias and fairness.
